Position Summary

Our post-secondary education client is seeking an accomplished and strategic financial executive to serve as its next Vice President, Finance and Administration / Chief Financial Officer.

As the chief financial and administrative officer and a member of the President’s Cabinet, the CFO provides executive leadership, strategic direction, and institutional stewardship for the school's financial operations, administrative services, public safety, and facilities.

This position ensures the responsible management of institutional resources while advancing the school's mission, vision, and strategic priorities. Serving as a trusted advisor to the President, the CFO helps shape institutional strategy, provides sound financial counsel, and partners with executive leadership to maintain the school's fiscal strength, operational effectiveness, and long-term sustainability.

The successful candidate will bring an institution-wide perspective and contribute to college-wide initiatives, organizational culture, governance, strategic planning, and executive decision-making.

Executive Leadership Philosophy

The school's Vice Presidents serve as institutional leaders first and functional leaders second. As members of the President’s Cabinet, they share responsibility for advancing the school's mission through collaborative leadership, strategic planning, innovation, and responsible stewardship of public resources.

The CFO will balance financial discipline with innovation, operational excellence with exceptional customer service, and long-term sustainability with the evolving needs of students, employees, and the community.

Success will be measured not only by the performance of assigned departments, but also by meaningful contributions to the overall effectiveness, reputation, and future of the college.
